## Understanding the Science of Change
In the digital age, mastering your habits is the ultimate competitive advantage. Based on the research of BJ Fogg and Charles Duhigg, we explore how small changes lead to massive results.

### The Fogg Behavior Model (B=MAP)
BJ Fogg posits that behavior (B) happens when Motivation (M), Ability (A), and a Prompt (P) come together. At The Brightway Library, we apply this to financial literacy. If you want to master 'The Psychology of Money', you don't need more willpower; you need a better system.

### Financial Literacy and Habit Stacking
James Clear’s 'Atomic Habits' introduces habit stacking. By pairing a new habit with an existing one—like reading 5 pages of 'Rich Dad Poor Dad' while drinking your morning coffee—you reduce the friction of starting. This is the core of our strategic vision for user growth.

### Why Environment Matters
Environment design is the secret to long-term success. If your goal is to stay updated with global news via our library, make this site your browser's homepage. This simple 'Prompt' (as per Fogg's model) ensures that you stay informed without needing high motivation.
